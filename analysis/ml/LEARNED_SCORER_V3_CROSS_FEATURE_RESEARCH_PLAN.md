# V3 cross-feature research plan

No cross network is implemented in this closure. A future controlled study may
compare the frozen MLP against explicit crosses such as threat×isolation,
threat×health, environment×provider mode, narrative×intent,
memory×repetition, fatigue×candidate intensity, and recoveryNeed×NO_ACTION.
These are hypotheses for TRAIN/VALIDATION-only experiments; TEST/HOLDOUT must
remain locked. Priority is high for threat×isolation and environment×provider,
medium for narrative×intent and memory×repetition, and unsupported until a
feature is confirmed present for any other interaction.
